# Washington Square survey audit

Initial audit: 15 November 2026. Peripheral enrichment: 16 November 2026. The bundled sample retains its Wordscape 0.4 baseline contract and is migrated to the current format by the app; earlier detail and terrain additions remain intact. Study area: 360 × 360 m. This document records coverage and evidence, not a claim of survey completeness.

## Image detail

The original 60 cm NAIP data was exported at 30 cm per pixel. Enlarging it to 300% enlarged the same information, so small benches and facade detail could not become sharper. The added NOAA photograph is genuine **25 cm source data**, exported at 25 cm per local pixel with the same geographic bounds. It is clearer but older: August 2022 versus May 2024. Neither can resolve all ground-level details hidden by tree crowns or buildings.

The city's 8 cm aerial service was located. The app links the official viewer; those tiles are not redistributed because an explicit dataset license was not established in the official service/catalog metadata. Google Maps remains an external comparison with its own attribution and terms. Source imagery was not AI-upscaled or sharpened.

## Mapped circulation addition

Thirteen actual OpenStreetMap park footways contribute 55 segments and 67 connections through shared source node IDs. Their north-up overlay was visually checked against the NOAA photograph in the existing 360 m frame. They form one connected mapped network. Centerlines are mapped; corridor widths are inferred. The 0.16 m surface offset is an authored rendering separation from the older lawn/promenade layers, not a measured step height. The source was retrieved on 15 November 2026 local time; map edit timestamps are not survey dates.

The extract contained no entrance-tagged nodes. No entrance, door location or doorway dimension was invented, and the absence of mapped entrances does not establish their real absence. Route connectivity is topological evidence, not a determination of public access, current passability or wheelchair suitability. The path network currently stops at its mapped extent and does not establish building-interior circulation.

The separate retained full-area extraction converts individual OSM buildings, three mapped building parts, streets, paths, trees and areas. It is an import example, not an automatic replacement for the hand-traced baseline. The Round 9 addition below uses only non-overlapping peripheral candidates; no duplicate building mass replaces an existing frontage. Buildings with mapped parts keep a parent outline as metadata in the full import; incomplete part coverage still needs review. Complex multipolygons and courtyard holes are explicitly omitted by this importer.

## Peripheral buildings and alley addition

Round 9 reuses the retained bounded OSM extraction captured at `2026-11-16T03:24:27.930514+00:00` (15 November local time). No network acquisition or model call was needed. The 326 successfully converted building/part candidates were compared with the preserved scene in its exact 360 m local frame. The accepted footprints were then overlaid on both the 2024 NAIP and finer 2022 NOAA images and visually checked against visible roofs.

The resulting **24 individual buildings** fill gaps around the outer blocks, including the mapped Filbert garage frontage, Stockton hospital mass and several smaller peripheral roofs. These are OSM building/address tags, not newly verified current business uses. Original OSM IDs and source tags are retained. Eighteen added buildings have mapped height tags; six use explicit estimates, including the converter's 3 m-per-mapped-level rule where available. None has a mapped facade color, roof color, wall material or roof-shape tag in this retained source. Facade/roof tones are therefore **authored display choices**, wall material remains unknown, and flat roof shapes retain their inferred evidence. New masses use a modeled level foundation on the existing DEM, not a measured floor elevation.

The deterministic selection uses an outer 70 m band, at least 15 m² of footprint area, no more than 0.01 m² of overlap with preserved building/landmark/road/park/path geometry, and at least 0.5 m clearance from existing building aggregates. It retains the 24 largest eligible footprints, then checks them against each other. The threshold and cap are authored scope choices, not survey standards. Of the other candidates, 88 overlap preserved geometry, 63 lie outside this peripheral selection, three are building parts, 13 are tiny clipped fragments, and 135 remain beyond the deliberate cap. Omission does not imply real-world absence.

Eleven named minor-road ways were also checked against **all** retained OSM building footprints, not just the 24 additions, plus the preserved scene. Only the two segments of **Via Ferlinghetti** clear those polygons at the importer's inferred corridor width. They are added together with their genuine shared-node connection. The other ten ways remain omitted because their inferred corridor envelopes intersect recorded buildings or grounds; no widths were fabricated as source measurements and no partial alley fragments were scattered into the scene. This is mapped connectivity, not a current access or accessibility assessment.

## What remains missing or uncertain

- **Building completeness:** many peripheral roofs, individual parcels, courtyards, additions, setbacks and entrances are omitted. Most building floor counts are explicitly inferred from scene height and neighborhood context. Most wall materials remain unknown. Window/door patterns are procedural interpretations.
- **Ground geometry:** real 2023 USGS bare-earth terrain is now captured at 5 m grid spacing, replacing the flat-ground assumption. Roof displacement differs between images; canopy centers are not trunks. Lawn edges and the walk are simplified. Fine retaining details, curbs and steps remain absent, and ground beneath buildings is interpolated.
- **Park furniture:** the model does not inventory every bench, lamp, trash bin, drinking fountain, sign, fence, chain, utility cover, irrigation head or curb ramp. The southern planting island exists, but its planting species and low perimeter fencing are not reconstructed.
- **Public art:** Franklin is included. Volunteer Firemen, Marini and other local memorial/fountain features remain omitted until location and scale are checked. Their existence alone does not justify guessing geometry.
- **Vegetation:** most species are unknown. The 2017 assessment records removals and historical conditions; those conditions are not applied as current health attributes. Four street trees use inventory species; the central poplar group has a cautious inferred match.
- **Streets:** lane counts, bus-stop extents, parking/loading rules, current signs and exact markings remain incomplete. Road objects span multiple official segments. Facts with a limited scope use notes or separate keys and do not imply a uniform limit across a whole street.
- **Time:** the world combines a 2024 plan sketch, 2022 detail image, 2023 lidar terrain, 2017/2018 historical evidence and data retrieved in 2026. It is not a single-date measured digital twin.

## Terrain addition

The public-domain USGS 3DEP 1 m raster from the CA_SanFrancisco_B23 project provides a complete native crop. Acquisition is recorded as 20 April 2023. The sampled world grid has 73 × 73 nodes at 5 m spacing, covering the same 360 m square. All 5,329 samples are present. Heights remain absolute NAVD88 (GEOID18) metres; the 10 m rendering reference is separately recorded. The sampled minimum is 11.728 m and maximum 49.788 m. There is no vertical exaggeration.

This materially improves the hillside context but does not establish building floor elevations. A flat architectural foundation placed on interpolated ground is a rendering interpretation; a road or lawn may follow the sampled surface. Legacy absolute placements must retain their meaning, while terrain-relative placements require an explicit choice. Parent links do not imply that a child changes vertical datum.

The project accuracy statistics apply to source testing, not each sampled point. A 5 m grid cannot faithfully capture small walls, curbs, terraces or stairs. No missing values were filled. Source metadata, a small native GeoTIFF, reproducible extraction script and full processing manifest are retained. See `docs/SOURCES.md` for provenance and limitations.

## Evidence and hierarchy contract

`observed` describes visible geometry; it does not make every dimension measured. Facts use `sourced` only with source IDs, `inferred` for supported estimates, `authored` for representative design choices, and `unknown` with a null value for missing knowledge.

Tower objects have the church as parent. Central tree groups and Franklin belong to the lawn; perimeter trees, benches, restroom and playground belong to the promenade. Marini trees belong to Marini Plaza. Crossing objects belong to their road. These are semantic relationships: all positions remain absolute world coordinates and a parent is not a transform.

The primary rendering keys are `building.levels`, `building.roofShape`, `building.material`, `building.color`, `building.roofColor`, `building.facade`, `tree.form`, `tree.species`, `road.direction`, `road.lanes`, `road.speedLimit`, and `fixture.type`. Other facts retain dimensions, historical context and segment scope without requiring the renderer to invent unsupported visual detail.

Recorded coverage should separate sourced values from inferred, observed and unknown facts. A numeric building height or floor estimate is not a documented measurement. Historical inferred species and partial street-segment limits remain unresolved at their wider scope. Counts describe what this document records, not how completely the real place has been surveyed.

## Verification

The downloaded NOAA crop was visually inspected and its original raster resolution checked as 0.25 × 0.25 m in EPSG:6339. Its 1440 × 1440 output matches the existing bounds. Both the enriched 0.2 sample and integrated 0.3 terrain sample pass parsing and data validation without errors or spatial warnings. Terrain extraction was reproduced from the retained native crop; checks confirmed 5,329 finite values, full 360 m coverage, correct source ID and recorded extrema. Independent corner/center checks confirmed orientation and native-raster alignment. Source records and coordinate manifests remain under `public/reference` for inspection and reproducibility.
